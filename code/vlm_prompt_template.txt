# VLM prompt templates (DRAFT, 2026-10-17; not final)
# One request per target frame (targets.csv from make_vlm_inputs.py). Images are sent in the order listed in
# targets.csv, each preceded by its caption (interleaved text and images). The question comes after the images.
# No numbers are given, not even the ego speed (decided 2026-10-17): the model judges from the images alone.
# Risk is deliberately NOT defined beyond the two ends of the scale: the study asks which SSM the model's own notion of
# risk resembles, so the prompt must not hint at time-to-collision or distance.

=================================================================================================================
LAYOUT "separate" (make_vlm_inputs.py --layout separate): 25 images, every camera captioned
=================================================================================================================
[SYSTEM]
You are an experienced driver. You are driving the car whose cameras took the following pictures.

[USER]
Below are pictures from your car's five cameras at 5 moments, taken 0.5 seconds apart and shown in time order. Moment 5 is now.

Moment 1 (2.0 s ago)
Front-left camera: <image>
Front camera: <image>
Front-right camera: <image>
Left-side camera: <image>
Right-side camera: <image>

Moment 2 (1.5 s ago)
Front-left camera: <image>
Front camera: <image>
Front-right camera: <image>
Left-side camera: <image>
Right-side camera: <image>

Moment 3 (1.0 s ago)
(the same five cameras)

Moment 4 (0.5 s ago)
(the same five cameras)

Moment 5 (now)
Front-left camera: <image>
Front camera: <image>
Front-right camera: <image>
Left-side camera: <image>
Right-side camera: <image>

How risky is the driving situation right now, at moment 5?
Give a risk score from 0 to 100, where 0 means no risk at all and 100 means a collision is about to happen.

Reply with JSON only, in this format:
{
  "risk": <integer from 0 to 100>,
  "main_hazard_camera": "front-left" | "front" | "front-right" | "left-side" | "right-side" | "none",
  "main_hazard": "<the road user or situation that matters most, in a few words>",
  "hazard_type": "rear-end" | "lane change or side" | "crossing or intersection" | "pedestrian or cyclist" | "oncoming" | "other" | "none",
  "reason": "<one sentence>"
}
