# VLM prompt template (DRAFT, 2026-10-17; not final)
# One request per target frame (results/vlm_inputs/targets.csv). The five step images are sent in time order,
# each preceded by its caption. {SPEED_LINE} is either empty (images only) or
# "Your speed right now is {ego_speed_kmh} km/h." (the speed the driver's speedometer shows: Waymo's reported ego
# speed at the target frame). Risk is deliberately NOT defined beyond the two ends of the scale: the study asks which
# SSM the model's own notion of risk resembles, so the prompt must not hint at time-to-collision or distance.

[SYSTEM]
You are an experienced driver. You are driving the car whose cameras took the following pictures.

[USER]
Below are 5 pictures from your car, taken 0.5 seconds apart and shown in time order: picture 1 was taken 2.0 seconds ago and picture 5 is now.
Each picture combines your five cameras: the top row shows front-left, front and front-right; the bottom row shows the left side and the right side. Each panel is labelled with its camera.
{SPEED_LINE}

How risky is the driving situation right now, in picture 5?
Give a risk score from 0 to 100, where 0 means no risk at all and 100 means a collision is about to happen.

Reply with JSON only, in this format:
{
  "risk": <integer from 0 to 100>,
  "main_hazard_camera": "front left" | "front" | "front right" | "side left" | "side right" | "none",
  "main_hazard": "<the road user or situation that matters most, in a few words>",
  "hazard_type": "rear-end" | "lane change or side" | "crossing or intersection" | "pedestrian or cyclist" | "oncoming" | "other" | "none",
  "reason": "<one sentence>"
}

Picture 1 (2.0 s ago): <image step 1>
Picture 2 (1.5 s ago): <image step 2>
Picture 3 (1.0 s ago): <image step 3>
Picture 4 (0.5 s ago): <image step 4>
Picture 5 (now): <image step 5>
